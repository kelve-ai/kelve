# Changelog

What changes in each release of kelve, for the people who run it. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org): until 1.0, a new minor version can change anything, and its notes say what.

## Unreleased

The first release, 0.1.0, will hold everything below.

- **One way to read.** Ask your agent to show you what it did, and kelve opens on the work itself: whole files, each idea it rests on marked beside the lines that use it.
- **Down to first principles.** Click an idea and its entry opens beside the work, from a shared knowledge graph; click what it rests on, and so on, to the ground.
- **Your agent does kelve's work,** on your own plan: kelve has no model and no key of its own.
- **Notes,** just for you, kept with the work, or for your agent to pick up the next time you message it.
- **The shared graph** grows as people's agents add what it doesn't have yet, each entry checked against its sources before anyone else reads it.
