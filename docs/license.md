# What the license allows

kelve is open source, under the [Apache License, Version 2.0](../LICENSE). This page puts it in plain words. Where this page and the license seem to differ, the license is what counts.

## In short

- You can use kelve, change it, and share it, for anything, at work too.
- If you share it, or something built from it, include the license and the [NOTICE](../NOTICE), and say what you changed.
- The license includes a license to any of our patents that kelve necessarily uses. It ends if you claim kelve infringes a patent.
- The license doesn't give you the right to use the name kelve, its logo or its mark, except to say where the software comes from. [brand/README.md](../brand/README.md#using-the-name-and-the-logo) says how they may be used.

## kelve's hosted service

The `kelve` app can use kelve's hosted service, kelve.ai, for verified scores and your page. The service's server isn't part of this repository, and it has terms of its own. The Apache License covers the software in this repository.

## Contributions

Contributions are licensed under the Apache License 2.0, as section 5 of the license says: see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Other people's code in kelve

kelve includes libraries other people made, under their own licenses: Rust crates in the app and command line, and React in the web app, mostly under MIT or Apache 2.0. [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) lists each with its license text, and goes wherever kelve goes: kelve serves it at `/notices.txt`, with its own license at `/license.txt`.
