# kelve's name and logo

The logo comes from the app's own look: warm paper, ink, Geist, and one mark, a small red square.

## Using the name and the logo

kelve's code is open source, under the Apache License 2.0. The kelve name, the logo and the mark aren't: they say something comes from kelve, and the license gives no right to use them (its section 6). The files here are for kelve's own pages, and for anyone writing about kelve.

- Use them, unchanged, to refer to kelve: in an article, a talk, a list of tools, or to say something works with kelve.
- Don't use them for something of your own, or in a way that says kelve made it or stands behind it. A fork of kelve takes another name and another mark.
- Don't change them: no other colours, no words added to the mark, nothing squashed or redrawn.

## Which logo to use

`logo/` has each one:

- **The lockup** (`lockup.svg`) wherever kelve is named: a page's header, a README, a slide. On a dark ground use `lockup-dark.svg`.
- **The mark** alone (`mark.svg`) where space is square or small, or where kelve is already named nearby: an avatar, a favicon, a corner.
- **The wordmark** alone (`wordmark.svg`) only in running text or a list of names, where a square would read as a bullet.
- **One colour** (`lockup-ink.svg`, `lockup-paper.svg`, `mark-ink.svg`, `mark-paper.svg`) only where a second colour can't print: a stamp, an engraving, a one-colour sticker.

The lockup is the app's top bar, drawn bigger: the word in Geist at 600 and -0.01em, the square 58% of the type's size, a gap as wide as the square, and the square centred on the line. Its text is outlined, so no file needs the font.

## Clear space and size

- Keep clear, on every side, a space as wide as the square. Nothing goes inside it, not even a rule.
- The lockup is never narrower than 56 px on screen (15.5 px type, as the top bar has it) or 14 mm in print.
- The mark is never smaller than 8 px.

## Don't

- Don't redraw, stretch, rotate or outline the mark, or round it more or less. Its corner is a fifth of its side.
- Don't set "kelve" in another face, in capitals, or with a capital K.
- Don't recolour the mark beyond the versions here, or put the red square on red, on a photograph, or on a busy ground.
- Don't add gradients, shadows, glows or textures, to the logo or around it.
